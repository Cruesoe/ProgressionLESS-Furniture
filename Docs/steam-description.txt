[h1]ProgressionLESS: Furniture[/h1]
[b]All the Progression goodness, less research.[/b]

[i]This is a fork of [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3771246586]Progression: Furniture[/url] by ferny. Huge thanks to ferny for the original mod and all the Progression work this builds on. For a really good modpack, check out [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3521297585]The Progression Modpack[/url].[/i]

[h2]What it does[/h2]
[list]
[*]Integrates furniture and decoration mods into vanilla progression, so each piece unlocks in its proper tech era.
[*]Floors, beds, tables, chairs, games, lights and electronics all have a place in the research tree, from tribal bedrolls to spacer furniture.
[*]Works with the rest of the Progression suite: other Progression mods that build on Tables, Chairs or Floors keep their links.
[/list]

[h2]Why "LESS"?[/h2]
Progression: Furniture adds up to [b]88 research projects[/b] depending on your mods. This fork groups them into [b]at most 24[/b] themed projects:
[list]
[*][b]Neolithic:[/b] Tables, Chairs, Floors, Columns and walls, Beds, Plant pots, Sarcophagus, Games
[*][b]Medieval:[/b] Cloth beds, Table games, Nursery, Architecture, Braziers and lamps, Coffin
[*][b]Industrial:[/b] Comfort furniture, Gaming tables, Chemfuel lights, Sports, Alarm clocks, Electric lights, Display light, Electronics, Advanced electronics
[*][b]Spacer:[/b] Spacer doors and frames
[/list]
Beds covers bedrolls, simple beds and animal beds, including Vanilla Furniture Expanded's simple beds. A few items return to the vanilla or mod research they came from, such as dressers and clocks to Complex furniture, and spacer furniture to Vanilla Furniture Expanded's Spacer research.

Everything stays in the same era and at the same stage within it, and still needs the other research it did before.

[h2]Compatibility[/h2]
Keeps all of the original's mod support, including:
[list]
[*]Royalty, Ideology, Biotech and Odyssey
[*]Vanilla Furniture Expanded (with the Additional Pack, Spacer and Spacer Addon), VFE Architect, Art, Classical, Settlers, VE Genetics and VQE Ancients
[*]Medieval Overhaul, Dark Ages crypts, Big Graves and Sarcophaguses, Medieval Deathrest Coffin and Animal Sarcophagus
[*]Big and Small Furniture (with More Medieval Overhaul Beds), Polyamory Beds, Advanced Poly Beds, More Animal Beds, Repair Bed and MyDogsHouse
[*]ReBuild Doors and Corners, Panels and Frames, Architecture of the Rim, Steve's Walls and Steve's Doors
[*]Ponpeco Kids Room and Sci-Fi, Erin's Decorations and Japanese Furniture, Victorian Decorations, Museums and Clocks
[*]Appliances Expanded, Helixien Gas, Lanterns Continued, Biomes Fossils, Deck 2, BallGames, Mahjong Table and Beach Stuff
[*]Flooring Floors, Outland More Floors, Oracle's Miscellania, Gerrymon Nautian Style and many more decoration mods
[*]Progression: Arsenal, Node Research and the rest of the Progression suite
[/list]

[b]Replaces the original. Don't use both.[/b]
